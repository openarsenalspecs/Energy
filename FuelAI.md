# FuelAI  
**The Route to Smarter Power**

FuelAI is an open-source global energy intelligence and navigation platform that helps users find the **cheapest way to power any journey**.

It combines **real-time energy price data**, **AI-driven routing**, and **multi-energy comparison** (gas, electricity, hydrogen, and more) into a single system designed for drivers, fleets, developers, and governments.

---

## 🚀 Overview

FuelAI goes beyond traditional fuel apps by providing:

- Multi-energy price comparison (gas, EV, hydrogen, etc.)
- AI-powered route optimization based on **lowest energy cost**
- Real-time and predictive energy pricing
- Open data infrastructure for global collaboration

---

## ⚡ Core Capabilities

- Compare **all major energy types** in one platform  
- Calculate **true cost per mile** for any vehicle  
- Navigate routes based on **lowest total energy cost**  
- Integrate **real-time + predicted pricing data**  
- Support **open-source development and APIs**
